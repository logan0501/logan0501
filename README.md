# Hi there, I'm Loganathan! 👋

## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Backend Engineer at Truemeds**, building distributed, event-driven systems in **Java/Spring Boot** and **Go**. Around 3 years in, my work sits mostly in logistics and supply-chain backends — courier allocation, delivery-date promises, and the asynchronous pipelines that keep them fed.

I care a lot about the unglamorous parts: idempotency, safe rollouts of changes to live decision systems, and making sure a job that runs at 3 AM can crash halfway through and resume without anyone waking up.

### 🛠️ What I Work On

- **Distributed systems** — event-driven services over SQS/SNS and Kafka, with an eye on failure modes rather than happy paths
- **Data pipelines** — nightly batch computation in Snowflake orchestrated with Airflow, landing in DynamoDB for low-latency serving
- **Safe rollouts** — shadow instrumentation so a scoring change can be measured against the live baseline before it ever affects a customer
- **Backend platform work** — bulk configuration tooling that moves ops teams off engineer-run DB scripts
